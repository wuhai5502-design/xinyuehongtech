Privacy Policy

How Shenzhen Xinyuehong Information Technology Co., Ltd. handles the information you send through this website — what is collected, why, where it is stored, how long, and how to have it removed.

Last updated 27 November 2026

Who is responsible for your data

The controller of the information described here is Shenzhen Xinyuehong Information Technology Co., Ltd., No. 38 Tian’an Road, Bantian Community, Longgang District, Shenzhen, Guangdong, China. Questions about this policy, or a request to access or delete your data, can be sent to sales@xinyuehongtech.com.

What the website collects

This website has no user accounts, no checkout and no advertising network. It collects information in three ways only:

  • The inquiry form. Name, company, email address, telephone or WhatsApp number, country, the product or application you select, and the message you write. The form is submitted to our inquiry endpoint and delivered as an email to our sales team.
  • Analytics. Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-QVGKHTQCZX) records page views, approximate location derived from the network, device and browser, referring page and a cookie identifier. It does not identify you as a person to us.
  • Direct contact. If you email us or open the WhatsApp link, the message and your contact details arrive through that channel. WhatsApp handles that conversation under its own privacy terms.

Why we use it

Inquiry details are used to answer your question, prepare a quotation, and follow up on a project you asked about. Analytics data is used to understand which pages are read and which are not, so the site can be improved. We do not sell your information, do not share it with advertising networks, and do not use it for automated decision-making.

Who receives it

  • Our sales team, as the recipient of every inquiry.
  • Our hosting and delivery providers, which serve the site and store the inquiry endpoint: GitHub Pages, Cloudflare and the inquiry API at xinhong.site.
  • Google Analytics, which processes site analytics on our behalf.

None of these providers is authorised to use your inquiry for its own marketing.

How long we keep it

Inquiry records are kept while the commercial relationship is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for warranty and accounting purposes. Analytics data is retained as configured in Google Analytics, up to 14 months. If you ask us to delete an inquiry, we remove it unless a legal obligation requires us to keep it.

Cookies

The only cookies set by this site come from Google Analytics. They can be blocked or deleted in your browser settings, and blocking them does not prevent you from using any part of the site. The site sets no marketing or retargeting cookies.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information you sent, ask for it to be corrected, ask for it to be deleted, or object to being contacted again. Write to sales@xinyuehongtech.com and we will respond within 30 days. Where the General Data Protection Regulation or UK GDPR applies, these rights are the ones we operate under, and you may also complain to your local supervisory authority.

Where the data is processed

Our company and sales team are in China, and our hosting and analytics providers operate internationally. Information you send may therefore be processed outside your country. We keep the amount of data collected to what an inquiry requires, and we do not transfer inquiry data to third parties for their own purposes.

Children

This site is a business-to-business website for procurement, engineering and operations staff. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If the way the site collects data changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top of the page will change. Questions about anything here can be sent to the email address above.
