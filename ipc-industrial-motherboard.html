Industrial Motherboard

Board-level options for kiosk and automation projects that need a specific form factor, I/O count and long-term supply.

An industrial motherboard is chosen when the enclosure, I/O set or product lifecycle cannot be met by an off-the-shelf system. Form factor, CPU generation, expansion slots and BIOS behaviour should be defined from the software requirements backwards, then agreed in writing with a supply commitment.

All industrial PCs

Configuration options

Points to agree before a quotation. Final availability, interfaces and ratings depend on the selected platform.

  • Form factor: ATX, Micro-ATX, Mini-ITX or 3.5-inch
  • CPU socket type or onboard processor and TDP
  • Memory type, slots and maximum capacity
  • PCIe, M.2, COM and USB expansion layout
  • BIOS/UEFI settings and boot behaviour
  • Long-term availability and revision control
