AUTHOR

Mark, Project Engineer

Project engineer at Shenzhen Xinyuehong Information Technology Co., Ltd., with ten years on kiosk and industrial-PC projects. Mark writes the technical guides published on this site: enclosure and thermal decisions, module selection, payment and identity integration, and what actually fails in 24/7 operation.

Who writes these guides: every technical article on xinyuehongtech.com carries Mark’s byline. He is a project engineer at the Shenzhen factory, not a marketing writer, and the drawings and photographs in the articles come from terminals built there.

What the work covers

  • Enclosure and thermal design for 24/7 terminals: fanless layouts, cabinet ambient rise, service access.
  • Module selection: printers, scanners, card readers, payment brackets, biometric sensors, and the SDK each one needs.
  • Platform choice: Android embedded builds against Windows x86 box PCs, decided from the peripheral SDK list.
  • Project delivery: specification review, sample validation, driver freezing, burn-in and FQC.
