Fanless Embedded Box PC

Blue fanless box PC with six DB9 serial ports and dual Wi-Fi antennas on the front panel, over a lower I/O strip carrying dual LAN, HDMI, VGA, four USB ports and audio.

The unit in the reference photo is a blue sheet-metal box PC with a finned passive heatsink and no fan. The front panel carries six DB9 serial ports labelled COM1 to COM6, two screw-mounted Wi-Fi antennas and a vented section; below them sit the DC input, VGA, HDMI, four USB ports, two RJ45 LAN ports, audio jacks and the power button. Because the connectors are brought out to the panel rather than left on a rear bracket, serial devices, printers, scanners and card readers can be plugged in without opening the cabinet.

Interfaces on this unit

Interfaces listed below are the ones visible on the reference photos. Port counts and levels for your build are confirmed in the specification review.

  • 6 x DB9 serial, COM1 to COM6
  • 2 x RJ45 LAN
  • HDMI and VGA
  • 4 x USB (USB 2.0 and 3.0 mixed)
  • Audio out and microphone in
  • 2 x Wi-Fi antennas on the front panel
  • DC input with power and reset buttons
