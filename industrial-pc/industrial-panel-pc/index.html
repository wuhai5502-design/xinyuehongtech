Industrial Panel PC

All-in-one panel computers combining a touchscreen, computing platform and I/O in one enclosure for operator stations and equipment HMIs.

A panel PC removes the separate screen, PC and cabling by integrating them behind one front panel. The decisions that matter are the touch technology, the brightness and optical treatment, the front-panel ingress rating and how the unit is mounted into a cabinet or wall cut-out.

All industrial PCs

Configuration options

Points to agree before a quotation. Final availability, interfaces and ratings depend on the selected platform.

  • Screen size and aspect ratio
  • Projected capacitive or resistive touch
  • Brightness, anti-glare and optical bonding
  • Front panel ingress protection rating
  • Flush panel mount, VESA or stand mounting
  • Operating system and I/O layout
