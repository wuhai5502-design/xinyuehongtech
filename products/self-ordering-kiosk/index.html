Before you request a quote

What POS or ordering software will be used?
The terminal is quoted as hardware plus platform, not as a POS. Your ordering application runs on Windows or Android and drives the screen, printer and payment device, and the cabinet and module set are then arranged around its requirements.
Is contactless payment handled by an external certified terminal?
In most projects, yes. The kiosk provides the mounting, power and cable route for a certified payment terminal, and the payment certification stays with that device rather than with the kiosk. An integrated reader is possible where your acquirer approves it.
What are the location, footprint and daily use requirements?
Counter-top units suit low counters and low volume; floor-standing units suit queues and busy sites. Give us the venue, the expected transactions per hour and the counter height, and the enclosure follows from those three answers.
