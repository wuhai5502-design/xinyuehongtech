One chassis, five counter environments

Border posts, bank halls, hotel lobbies, cinema foyers and supermarket service desks ask for the same core actions: identify the customer, take a document or a payment, print something back. This terminal is built as the common hardware answer to those four actions, so a project can be quoted once and then adapted by module rather than by cabinet. The illuminated deck and the LED base are there for attended lobbies where the unit has to read as furniture, not as a machine.

One cabinet, several industries

This is the largest terminal in the range and the one specified when a project cannot yet say which industry it will end up in. The 32-inch 2560 × 1440 portrait screen carries a full application rather than a tile menu, the scanner is set into an illuminated window below the display, and the card reader bay handles IC, ID and bank cards with a national ID reader where the module supports it.

The QHD resolution matters at this size. At arm's length a 32-inch 1080p panel shows visible pixels on small form text, which is exactly what a passport number, a bank account field or a hotel key-code screen contains. The higher pixel density keeps those fields legible without enlarging the interface.

The body is brushed stainless steel with a tempered glass counter deck, chosen because the unit stands in public halls where the front surface is touched, leaned on and cleaned hundreds of times a day. Powder-coated steel marks and dulls at that level of use; stainless steel does not. Modules are accessible from a separate lockable service door, so maintenance does not expose the payment bay.

Before you request a quote

Is the 2560 × 1440 screen necessary, or is 1080p enough?
For a tile-based kiosk application, 1080p is enough. For an application showing forms, account details or document previews at arm's length, the higher resolution keeps small text legible. The screen is one of the reasons this model is specified for immigration and banking flows.
What can be added to the module bay?
A passport MRZ reader, fingerprint sensor, cash acceptor and recycler, NFC pad, second display and A4 printing are all available. Each changes the internal layout and the service access, so the final module set is drawn before production rather than added afterwards.
Does the stainless steel body suit outdoor use?
No. It is a public-hall terminal, not an outdoor one. Rain, dust ingress and direct sun are not part of this cabinet's specification; for kerbside or lane-side installs, the parking payment terminal with a weather-resistant front is the appropriate model.
